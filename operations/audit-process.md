# Audit Process

## Overview

This document defines the audit process for TrimAtlas Product OS systems, workflows, and operational activities.

## Audit Goals

- Verify process compliance
- Identify improvement opportunities
- Maintain operational transparency
- Support continuous quality improvement

## Audit Areas

### System Audit

Review:

- System configurations
- Access controls
- Operational practices

### Process Audit

Evaluate:

- Documented workflows
- Change history
- Review procedures

### Security Audit

Check:

- Security practices
- Data protection measures
- Risk management controls

## Audit Workflow

1. Define audit scope
2. Collect required information
3. Review findings
4. Document results
5. Apply improvements

## Continuous Improvement

Audit results should guide better practices and stronger operational processes for TrimAtlas Product OS.
