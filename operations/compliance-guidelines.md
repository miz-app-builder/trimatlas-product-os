# Compliance Guidelines

## Overview

This document defines compliance practices for TrimAtlas Product OS operations and development.

## Compliance Goals

- Maintain responsible system operation
- Protect user and business information
- Follow required standards and processes
- Improve accountability

## Key Areas

### Data Protection

Ensure:

- Appropriate data handling
- Secure access controls
- Responsible information usage

### Process Compliance

Maintain:

- Documented workflows
- Review procedures
- Change records

### Security Compliance

Follow:

- Security guidelines
- Access management practices
- Risk management processes

## Review Process

Compliance practices should be reviewed regularly to identify gaps and improvement opportunities.

## Continuous Improvement

Compliance requirements should evolve with TrimAtlas Product OS growth and operational needs.
