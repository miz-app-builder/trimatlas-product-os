# Cost Management

## Overview

This document defines cost management practices for TrimAtlas Product OS operations.

## Cost Management Goals

- Optimize resource usage
- Maintain predictable operating costs
- Improve financial visibility
- Support sustainable product growth

## Cost Areas

### Infrastructure Costs

Track:

- Compute resources
- Storage usage
- Network consumption

### Operational Costs

Monitor:

- Service subscriptions
- Tooling expenses
- Maintenance requirements

### Development Costs

Consider:

- Engineering effort
- Technical improvements
- Long-term maintenance impact

## Review Process

Costs should be reviewed regularly to identify:

- Optimization opportunities
- Unnecessary expenses
- Resource efficiency improvements

## Continuous Improvement

Cost management practices should evolve alongside TrimAtlas Product OS growth.
