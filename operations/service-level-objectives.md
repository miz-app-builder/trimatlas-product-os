# Service Level Objectives

## Overview

This document defines reliability objectives for TrimAtlas Product OS services.

## Objectives

### Availability

Maintain reliable access to product services and workflows.

### Performance

Ensure acceptable response times and efficient system behavior.

### Reliability

Reduce failures and improve recovery capabilities.

## Service Indicators

Track:

- Availability metrics
- Error rates
- Response times
- Recovery time
- User-impacting incidents

## Review Process

Service objectives should be reviewed regularly to:

- Identify reliability gaps
- Improve operational practices
- Align technical performance with product goals

## Continuous Improvement

SLOs should evolve as TrimAtlas Product OS services and user needs grow.
