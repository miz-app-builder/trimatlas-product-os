# Architecture Decision Records

## Overview

This document defines the approach for recording important architecture decisions in TrimAtlas Product OS.

## Purpose

Architecture Decision Records (ADRs) help teams:

- Document technical choices
- Preserve decision context
- Improve future decision making
- Maintain architectural consistency

## ADR Structure

Each decision record should include:

- Decision title
- Context and problem
- Considered options
- Final decision
- Expected consequences

## Decision Process

### 1. Identify Need

Document the technical challenge or opportunity.

### 2. Evaluate Options

Review possible solutions based on:

- Technical impact
- Business requirements
- Maintainability
- Risk

### 3. Record Decision

Capture the selected approach and reasoning.

### 4. Review Later

Update decisions when requirements or architecture evolve.

## Continuous Improvement

Architecture decisions should remain transparent and aligned with TrimAtlas Product OS growth.
