# Governance

## Overview

This document defines the governance model for TrimAtlas Product OS.

## Governance Goals

- Maintain clear project direction
- Support transparent decision making
- Encourage responsible contributions
- Keep the project aligned with product goals

## Decision Making

Important decisions should consider:

- Product impact
- Technical feasibility
- User needs
- Long-term sustainability

## Roles and Responsibilities

Project participants should:

- Follow contribution guidelines
- Maintain quality standards
- Communicate decisions clearly
- Support continuous improvement

## Documentation Ownership

Documentation should be maintained as the project evolves to ensure accuracy and usefulness.

## Future Evolution

Governance practices may expand as TrimAtlas Product OS grows and the community develops.
