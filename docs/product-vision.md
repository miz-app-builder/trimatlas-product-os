# TrimAtlas Product Vision

## Vision

TrimAtlas aims to provide a structured product operating system that helps teams design, build, manage, and scale digital products efficiently.

## Mission

Create a unified foundation for product strategy, development workflows, documentation, and continuous improvement.

## Core Principles

- Product-first thinking
- Scalable architecture
- Clear documentation
- Fast iteration
- Continuous improvement

## Product Direction

The TrimAtlas Product OS will evolve into a central workspace for managing product ideas, requirements, technical decisions, and execution workflows.
