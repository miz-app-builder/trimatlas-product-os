# Roadmap Management

## Overview

This document defines the process for managing the TrimAtlas Product OS roadmap.

## Roadmap Goals

- Align product direction with user needs
- Prioritize valuable improvements
- Communicate future plans clearly
- Support predictable delivery

## Roadmap Process

### Discovery

Identify:

- User problems
- Market opportunities
- Product improvements

### Prioritization

Evaluate:

- Business impact
- User value
- Technical feasibility
- Required effort

### Planning

Define:

- Timeline expectations
- Dependencies
- Success criteria

### Review

Regularly update roadmap priorities based on feedback, metrics, and strategic goals.

## Continuous Improvement

The roadmap should evolve as TrimAtlas Product OS grows and user requirements change.
